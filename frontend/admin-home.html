What this account is for

The role of an admin on CityCare.

An admin account represents the municipal authority side of CityCare. While citizens use their accounts to report civic issues — potholes, garbage accumulation, broken streetlights, water problems and more — admin accounts exist to receive those reports, act on them, and keep citizens informed of progress.

Every complaint submitted by any citizen, from any location, becomes visible here. Nothing is filtered by who submitted it — an admin sees the full picture of civic issues across the city in one place.

What you can do here

Features available to an admin account.

View every complaint city-wide — each entry shows a photo, category, the citizen's submitted location, and when it was reported, all in one table.

Update complaint status — move a complaint through Pending, In Progress, Resolved, or Rejected as work on it progresses, so citizens can track it.

Assign a department — route each issue to the municipal department responsible for resolving it.

Monitor totals at a glance — the Dashboard's summary cards show how many complaints are pending, in progress, or resolved across the whole city.

Search and filter — quickly find a specific complaint by location, category, or keyword.
